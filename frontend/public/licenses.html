1. Fonts

DAB's pages and app use two font families made by IBM, served from DAB's own server: IBM Plex Sans Thai and IBM Plex Mono. Both are © IBM Corp. and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which lets anyone use, share and change them on conditions that include keeping IBM's copyright notice and the licence with every copy. DAB serves them unchanged. PDF reports exported from DAB may embed the characters they use from IBM Plex Sans Thai; the licence's requirement to stay under the same licence does not apply to documents created with the fonts.

Licence text and IBM's copyright notices: OFL-1.1-IBM-Plex.txt (plain text, in English).

The fonts in the landing-page video

The 3D lettering in the background video on the landing page was rendered from outlines converted from these fonts. Converted outlines count as a modified version under the licence, and IBM reserves the name “Plex” for its own fonts, so DAB's versions are named DAB Render Sans Thai and DAB Render Mono. They remain under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 with IBM's copyright notices, and IBM has not reviewed or endorsed them. They are not sent to your browser; only the finished video is.
